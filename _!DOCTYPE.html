BSMS Middle School Athletes Safe After Illinois Bus Rollover

Published: September 9, 2026

A school bus carrying the Benton-Scales Mound-Shullsburg (BSMS) middle school football team and coaching staff was involved in a rollover accident on Tuesday afternoon, September 8, 2026.

The incident occurred on Route 78 north of Chelsea Road near Stockton, Illinois, while the team was traveling to an away football game. According to report logs from the Jo Daviess County Sheriff's Office, the bus overturned during the journey.

Injuries and Emergency Response

School district officials confirmed that all student athletes and staff members were safely accounted for and able to walk away from the vehicle. However, the bus driver and three students were transported to a local hospital for further evaluation of minor injuries. They were treated and released later that evening.

First responders assisted at the scene, and uninjured students were safely reunited with their families at the Stockton Fire Department. Personal items left behind on the bus were recovered and held at Scales Mound School for student pickup.

School District Actions

In response to the emotional distress caused by the accident, the cooperative school districts made counselors available across their campuses the following morning to support affected students, players, and family members.
